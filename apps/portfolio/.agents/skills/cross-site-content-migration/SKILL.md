# Migrate cross-site content

1. Identify authoritative source and destination repositories and read their
   instructions, public documentation, release metadata, and relevant source.
2. Classify material as public, internal, outdated, duplicated, personal,
   licensed, or uncertain. Do not publish internal material by default.
3. Reconcile names, versions, commands, URLs, compatibility, and deprecations.
4. Adapt content for the destination reader, preserve Sakura's voice where the
   source is personal, and link to canonical documentation for full procedures.
5. Replace local paths and source-only instructions with public URLs or
   reader-facing paths.
6. Confirm asset provenance and licensing and preserve required attribution.
7. Mark genuinely uncertain behavior as unverified; never turn inference into
   fact.
8. Preserve route compatibility or report required redirects and reciprocal
   link updates.
9. Follow the destination project's authoring and validation skills.

Do not migrate secrets, private downloads, unpublished roadmaps,
security-sensitive details, personal data, or internal-only content. Do not edit
another repository or remote service unless it is explicitly in scope.
